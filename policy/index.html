Privacy Policy for Subway Runner

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the game application and website Subway Runner, developed and operated by Rajveer Nanadikar at Antify Studio.

Website: https://subwayfers.vercel.app

Application Installation: https://subwayfers.vercel.app/install

1. Data Collection and Usage

Subway Runner is designed with privacy in mind. We do not require users to create an account, log in, or submit personal identification details (such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses) to play the game.

  • Local Game Storage: Game progress, unlocked characters, audio settings, high scores, and accumulated virtual coins are stored locally on your device using Web Storage (localStorage). This data remains on your local device and is never transmitted to or stored on external servers.
  • Device & Network Data: Standard access logs (such as IP addresses, web browser types, and operating system information) may automatically be collected by our hosting provider (Vercel) for maintenance, security, and performance analytics.

2. In-App Purchases and Gameplay

Subway Runner does not require real-money payments or paid subscriptions to access core gameplay features, characters, or upgrades. All in-game coins and unlocked items are earned solely through gameplay achievements.

3. Optional Developer Contributions (Buy Me a Coffee)

A "Buy Me a Coffee" section is hosted at https://subwayfers.vercel.app/install to allow users to voluntarily support the game creator, Rajveer Nanadikar.

  • Optional Nature: Contributions made to rajveer-nanadikar@fam are strictly optional and voluntary. No gameplay content, features, or functionality are locked behind payment.
  • Payment Handling: Financial transactions are processed independently via standard payment provider interfaces. Subway Runner and Antify Studio do not collect, process, or retain sensitive payment details or banking information on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services and Libraries

The web application uses standard third-party content distribution networks (CDNs) to load graphic processing libraries (such as Three.js). These third-party services operate under their respective privacy policies and may collect technical header data necessary to serve file requests.

5. Application Package (APK) Installation

Users downloading or installing the Android Application Package (APK) via https://subwayfers.vercel.app/install are granted access strictly for personal entertainment. The application requires only basic runtime permissions necessary for local WebGL rendering and audio playback.

6. Children's Privacy

Subway Runner does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Because the game collects no personal identity data, children can safely enjoy the application without disclosing personal information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any adjustments or updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top.
